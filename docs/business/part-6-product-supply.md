## What this tells us (first reading)

- **Many products, many suppliers/subcontractors per product.** Buy price differs by supplier and job.
- **Storage / warehouse balance — three situations, set per job:**
  1. **Transport or sale only:** we do not keep any storage balance (not our product), but **the storage places still matter** as loading and discharge locations.
  2. **We are responsible for the storage balance** (by agreement): we record what goes in and out of the storage for that job.
  3. **The product is ours:** we keep its balance.
- **Closing a storage at the end of a job:** the balance must be brought to **zero**; the remainder is **loss / evaporation**.
  - Total loss = route losses (in transport) + storage loss.
  - If total loss ≤ the agreed **forgiveness** (allowance) → no loss is charged.
  - If total loss > forgiveness → **only the surplus** is the loss, valued **at the agreed rate** (the fine rate mentioned before).
- **Agreements are verbal;** the owner tells employees the buy rate and sell rate. **Employees must never see the profit or be able to work out the margin per job.** → Profit, margins and the price pair (buy vs sell / customer price vs driver price) must be visible only to the owner and roles he allows; staff entering data see only the one price they need.
- **Product cost:** sometimes transport is included in the supplier price; sometimes we buy at one price and transport at another price — then **product cost = purchase + transport + other expenses** (landed cost).
