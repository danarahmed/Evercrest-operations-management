## What this tells us (first reading)

- **No fixed main suppliers or subcontractors;** the list grows with the jobs. **Most suppliers are transporters**, and there can be very many. → Adding a partner must be quick (name + phone is enough), with search and duplicate warnings.
- **Paying suppliers/transporters is not per invoice:** sometimes an **advance**, sometimes **part of what we owe**. Always **cash**. → Payments go against the partner's **running account balance**, not against specific invoices (open-account style). Optional link to a claim when there is one.
- **Documents:** transporters usually bring no invoice of their own; **our office staff prepare the claim/settlement** from the documents they bring back. Other expenses may or may not have a receipt. → Expenses without a receipt must be allowed (with a reason / approval), receipts attachable when they exist.
- **Overheads:** the company has all the usual costs.
  - Some, like **salaries and rent**, should go to jobs **automatically**.
  - Costs that cannot be identified with a job are **shared across the jobs that were active at that time**.
  → Overhead allocation: monthly, spread over the jobs active in that month (basis to confirm), so job profit = direct profit − its share of overheads.
