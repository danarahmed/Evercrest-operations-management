## What this tells us (first reading)

- **Rules set by the manager, applied by employees:** employees process the files from the sites; the owner/manager **sets the rules** (prices, allowances, fees, advances, limits). Employees may **accept anything that is within the rules** without asking. Rules must be **flexible and dated** — they change often (even every few days).
  → Anything outside the rules (different price, larger advance, fee not in the job's card, fine waived…) becomes an **exception that needs the manager's approval**; inside the rules it flows through.
- **No payment without permission** — today given verbally. → In the system, permission is an explicit approval recorded with who/when, before money leaves a cash box (within limits set by the owner).
- **Owner asks me to design the controls:** faster and more accurate work **and** prevention of cheating.
- **Accounting errors today:** employees finish a settlement → accountant types journals by hand → mistakes, slow to find.
  → **The system must create the journal entries itself** from every operational action (trip, advance, fee, fine, claim, settlement, payment, exchange, overhead share), each to its proper account. **The accountant reviews and accepts** (audit), not types.
- **Owner's instruction:** think like a CFO for all such parts (also in other parts).
