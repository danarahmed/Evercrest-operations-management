## How each chapter is written

Every chapter answers the same questions, in the same order:

1. What the step adds, in one minute.
2. Which of your problems it solves, with the part of your questionnaire it comes from.
3. Who does each task.
4. How to use it, in the order the work happens, with pictures.
5. Where everything is found.
6. What the books record, and when.
7. What can go wrong and how to correct it.
8. A short "try it" checklist.
9. What is not built yet.

## Three rules the whole system follows

- **Nothing is deleted.** A mistake is corrected, and the correction is recorded with who did it, when, and why. Money already moved is corrected by a reversal, never by editing.
- **Money needs two people above a limit.** Payments above the limit, fine waivers and write-offs wait for a second person in Approvals. Nobody approves their own request.
- **One fact lives in one place.** A trip's figures live on the trip and are calculated from the job's rule card. Every screen reads the same calculation, so the trip grid, the claim and the books always agree.
