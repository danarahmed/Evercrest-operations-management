# Glossary

The words the system uses, in the three languages, exactly as they appear on the screens.

| English | العربية | کوردی | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | العمل | کار | One piece of work for one customer, e.g. "Diesel to Latakia, November". Everything else hangs on a job. |
| Job type | نوع العمل | جۆری کار | A template for a kind of work: the parts and steps a new job starts with. Set up in Setup → Jobs & documents. |
| Part (of a job) | جزء | بەش | Something a job can have switched on: Transportation, Advances, Billing, Expenses, Products, Field work, Documents. Each one adds its own tab or button to the job. |
| Step | خطوة | هەنگاو | One line of a job's checklist, ticked off in the Steps tab. A required step must be done before completion. |
| Document type | نوع المستند | جۆری بەڵگەنامە | The name of a paper the company handles: Manifest, Discharge certificate, Delivery note. |
| Document rule | قاعدة المستند | یاسای بەڵگەنامە | Says which paper is required, for which job type, customer or contract, on each trip or on the job, and before completion or financial close. |
| Custom field | حقل مخصص | خانەی تایبەت | An extra fact some jobs must carry, such as a customer's PO number or a field permit number. |
| Contract | عقد | گرێبەست | A customer's agreement. Jobs can be linked to it and papers can be required for it. Optional. |
| Team | الفريق | تیم | The people working on a job besides the responsible person. Being on a team gives no extra rights. |
| My jobs | أعمالي | کارەکانی من | The jobs where you are the responsible person or on the team. |
| Project | مشروع | پڕۆژە | A group of jobs of one customer. Optional. |
| Completion | الإكمال | تەواوکردن | The work is done. Required papers and steps must be in before a job can be marked completed. |
| Financial close | الإغلاق المالي | داخستنی دارایی | All money of the job is settled. The last status of a job; papers "needed before financial close" must be in by then. |
| Rule card | بطاقة القواعد | کارتی ڕێسا | The job's prices and rules: job-wide rules (fine, customer price, demurrage, advance limit) and prices by route. Shown on the job's Rules tab. |
| Version | نسخة | وەشان | A rule card change from a date. Trips use the version in force on their loading date. |
| Route | الطريق | ڕێگا | Where trucks load and discharge. A route can have its own price, reward and allowance. |
| Prices by route | الأسعار حسب الطريق | نرخ بەپێی ڕێگا | A route's own carrier price from a date, with its reward and allowance if they differ. One price for all carriers on that route. |
| Fee | الرسوم | خەرجی | Visa, insurance, guarantee, customs and similar, ticked on the trips that had it. |
| Kinds of fees | أنواع الرسوم | جۆرەکانی خەرجی | The list of fees set up once in Setup: usual amount, per trip or per ton, who pays it at the time, and who carries it in the end. |
| Pay fees | دفع الرسوم | پارەدانی خەرجییەکان | Pays the fees the company still owes, many trips in one payment from a cash box. |
| Fees to return to customers | رسوم تُعاد إلى الزبائن | خەرجی کە بۆ کڕیاران دەگەڕێنرێتەوە | A fee the customer paid directly and the carrier gave back: the company owes it to the customer. |
| Trip | الرحلة | گەشت | One truck carrying one load under a job. |
| Paper not coming | المستند لن يصل | بەڵگەکە نایەت | A paper that will never arrive, excused with a reason and the owner's approval. It then counts as complete. |
| Manifest | المنفيست | منفیست | The trip's paper and its identity. One manifest number is used once per job and is paid once. |
| Loaded | محمل | بارکراو | Quantity loaded, from the loading sheet. |
| Discharged | مفرغ | بەتاڵکراو | Quantity delivered, from the storage's list or the driver's papers. |
| Net | صافي | صافی | The lower of loaded and discharged. The carrier is paid on net. |
| Shortage | النقص | نقص | Loaded minus discharged, when the truck delivered less. |
| Allowance | السماح | ڕێگەپێدان | The shortage the carrier is not fined for, set on the rule card. |
| Fine | غرامة | غرامە | Shortage beyond the allowance × the fine price. Comes off the carrier's pay. |
| Fine waiver | التنازل عن الغرامة | لێخۆشبوونی غرامە | Part or all of a fine forgiven, after approval. Never an edit of the fine. |
| Reward | المكافأة | پاداشت | An amount per trip on top of the freight. |
| Advance | سلفة | سلف | Money paid to the driver or transporter at loading. Deducted when the trip is claimed. |
| Advance limit | حد السلفة | سنووری سلف | Advances up to this need no approval. Above it they go to Approvals. |
| Carrier | الناقل / السائق | ناقل / سایەق | Whoever drives or arranges the truck: a transporter, or a driver working directly with the company. |
| Transporter | الناقل | ناقل | A company or person who provides trucks and drivers. |
| Direct | مباشر | ڕاستەوخۆ | A driver working with the company with no transporter. |
| Estimated | تقديرية | خەمڵێنراو | A trip not yet discharged: its figures assume no shortage. |
| Confirmed | مؤكدة | دڵنیاکراو | A discharged trip: its figures are final. |
| Cut held for the transporter | مقتطع محتجز للناقل | بڕاوی گیراو بۆ ناقل | An amount cut from a driver on a trip and held until a transporter asks for it. Given back (all or part, after approval) or closed into income. |
| Held cuts | المقتطعات المحتجزة | بڕاوە گیراوەکان | The list of trips with a cut still held, under Claims. |
| Give back | إعادة | گەڕاندنەوە | Paying a transporter part or all of the held cuts he asks for, in cash or to his account; the rest of each trip's cut becomes the company's income. |
| Deduction kept by the company | خصم تحتفظ به الشركة | لێبڕینی کۆمپانیا | An amount taken from the carrier on a trip as the company's own income, e.g. claim handling. |
| Claim | مطالبة | مطالەبە | The list of trips a carrier brings papers for, and the money owed for them. |
| Claimant | صاحب المطالبة | داواکار | Whoever brought the papers. The trips' money goes to the claimant, whoever the transporter at loading was. |
| Site settlement | تسوية موقع | حیسابی شوێن | Drivers paid at a site with the site's own figures, sent back as a sheet and compared with the system. |
| Cash box | الصندوق | سندوق | A safe or bank account money is paid from. Each one holds one currency. |
| Approvals | الموافقات | پەسەندکردنەکان | The list of money actions waiting for a second person. |
| Payment limit | حدود الموافقة على المدفوعات | سنووری پەسەندکردن بۆ پارەدان | Payments above this amount need approval. |
| Partner account | حسابات الأطراف | هەژماری لایەنەکان | A customer's, transporter's or driver's running balance and its history. |
| Journal | اليومية | ڕۆژنامە | Every booking in the accounts. Nothing in it is ever edited. |
| Margin | الهامش | پەراوێز | What the customer pays minus what the trip costs. Only people with the profit permission see it. |
